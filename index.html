Privacy Policy

A clear explanation of how our Expense Tracker handles information when you use the application.

🛡️
Designed with privacy in mind

Our app does not require you to create an account, provide a username, or enter your email address. Your expense information is handled according to the app's actual storage and technical configuration.

Effective Date: October 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Expense Tracker. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when you use our application. We aim to respect your privacy and be transparent about our information practices.

2. Information We Collect

Expense Tracker does not require users to register an account or provide a username, email address, or password.

Depending on the features you use and how the app is configured, the application may handle:

  • Expense amounts and transaction descriptions.
  • Expense categories and transaction dates.
  • Budget limits, totals, and spending summaries.
  • App settings and preferences.
  • Technical or diagnostic information, if collected by the app or its service providers.

Please do not enter sensitive personal or financial information that the app does not need.

3. How We Use Information

Information handled by the app may be used to:

  • Record and organize your expenses.
  • Calculate spending totals and budget summaries.
  • Display transaction history and expense categories.
  • Save preferences and support app functionality.
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve reliability.

4. Data Storage

Expense information may be stored locally on your device or processed using a backend service, depending on the application's implementation.

If a cloud database such as Supabase is enabled, relevant data may be transmitted to and stored by that service. Please review the app's actual configuration to determine where your information is stored.

Supabase Privacy Policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information to third parties. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the application or when disclosure is required by law.

We do not claim that information is never transmitted to third parties; this depends on the services and features enabled in the app.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information handled by the application against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. However, no electronic storage system or method of transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

The retention of expense records depends on how the app stores data. Records stored locally may remain on your device until they are deleted or the app's data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Cloud-stored records may require a separate deletion process.

If you need assistance with personal information handled by the app, contact the developer using the contact details provided in the app's store listing.

8. Children's Privacy

The application is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without any authorization required by applicable law. If you believe personal information has been collected improperly, please contact the developer.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the handling of information by Expense Tracker, contact the application developer through the contact information provided in the app's store listing.
